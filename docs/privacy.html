Echo Loop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Echo Loop ("the app", "the game") is a puzzle-platformer for Android and iOS by Tyler Conley. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: the app does not collect, share or sell any personal information.

Information the app does not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking tools are included.
  • The app does not request device permissions such as location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.
  • The app does not connect to the internet or send any data to me or to third parties.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • The player name you choose to type in (used for the high score table). You may use a nickname.
  • Your game progress: current level, completed levels, current score and the top-10 high scores.
  • Your sound on/off setting.

This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising and purchases

The app contains no ads and no in-app purchases.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
